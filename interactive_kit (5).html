Exercise 6: Discussion Questions (Future Continuous Focus)

Discuss the questions. Click "Show Suggested Answers" to see Future Continuous examples. Immediate results will appear.

1. To see the sights: If you visit New York next summer, what will you be doing while you are seeing the sights? Will you be taking photos or visiting museums?
"I will be visiting museums and will be taking photos while I see the sights."
2. Sparks fly: Imagine a romantic trip next week. What will the couple be doing when sparks fly between them?
"They will be walking in Central Park when sparks fly."
3. To get carried away: If you get carried away with planning, what will you be checking or booking while the trip is happening?
"I will be booking extra tours while I get carried away."
4. To turn out (to be): While the trip turns out to be successful, what will the travelers be experiencing during the week?
"They will be enjoying the success while the trip turns out well."
5. To check (something): While you check the train tickets online, what else will you be doing at the same time?
"I will be comparing prices while I check the tickets."
6. To cancel (on someone): If someone cancels on the group, what will the others be doing instead during that time?
"We will be traveling while she cancels on us."
7. A third wheel: When a third wheel joins the couple, what will everyone be doing during the evening in the city?
"They will be touring the city while the third wheel joins."
8. To get serious: As the relationship gets serious, what will the couple be planning for their future trips?
"They will be confirming future trips while the relationship gets serious."
9. Stand someone up: If someone stands someone up on a date in New York, what will the person who was waiting be doing at that moment?
"The person will be sitting at the cafe and will be looking at the door while being stood up."
